## 2. Forward-Looking Pipeline

### 2.1 Organizational Structure

FIL GTM LLC operates a unified go-to-market function for paid Filecoin services, beginning with **Fil One** and the **Filecoin Developer Toolkit** (formerly Filecoin Onchain Cloud).

The organization is designed to coordinate customer acquisition, product adoption, and revenue generation while minimizing administrative overhead.

| Function | Responsibility |
| :--- | :--- |
| Enterprise Sales & Business Development | Dedicated team responsible for direct enterprise sales and commercial partnerships |
| Brand, Content, Events & Design | Shared services supporting all products |
| Product Marketing | Product-specific positioning, messaging, and campaigns |
| Sales Engineering | Technical support for enterprise sales, integrations, and migrations |
| Account Management & Support | Product-specific customer onboarding, retention, and support |
| Back-Office Administration | Accounting, tax, and administrative services provided by Filecoin Foundation |

This structure enables FIL GTM LLC to concentrate its resources on generating paid demand for Filecoin services.

### 2.2 Pipeline Overview

FIL GTM LLC maintains two distinct pipelines, each serving a different audience and commercial objective.

| Pipeline | Target Audience | Primary Objective | Measurement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fil One Revenue Pipeline | Enterprise buyers and channel partners | Generate paid enterprise storage contracts | ARR, open deals, closed deals, FPV |
| Developer Toolkit Adoption Pipeline | Developers, AI builders, application teams, existing IPFS users | Drive direct integration and adoption of Filecoin storage | Developer adoption, paid usage, FPV |

#### Fil One Revenue Pipeline

Fil One is the primary revenue pipeline.

It targets enterprise customers through four commercial channels:

1. **Partner Books:** Existing customer portfolios accessed through commercial partners.
2. **Data Center & Neocloud:** Data center operators and emerging cloud infrastructure providers.
3. **End Customers via Partner:** Enterprise customers acquired through resellers, distributors, and technology partnerships.
4. **End Customers Direct:** Enterprise customers acquired through FIL GTM LLC's direct sales team.

Deals are tracked from initial prospecting through negotiation and closing.

Paid storage transactions settle through registered payer/operator pairs and contribute to the Filecoin Pay Volume (FPV) attributed to this Orchestrator.

#### Filecoin Developer Toolkit Adoption Pipeline

The Filecoin Developer Toolkit, including Filecoin Pin, focuses on developers building applications that integrate Filecoin storage directly.

The target audience includes:

- Application developers integrating storage directly into their products.
- AI and autonomous agent builders.
- Existing IPFS users transitioning to paid storage.
- Developers exploring Filecoin infrastructure without enterprise contracts.

This pipeline is primarily evaluated through adoption and product usage rather than enterprise contract closures.

Eligible paid usage contributes to FPV once the corresponding payer/operator pairs have been registered.
